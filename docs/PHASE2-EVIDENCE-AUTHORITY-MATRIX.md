## Gate interpretation

The matrix is structurally complete for admission review. The Arc-to-GenLayer
source policy and no-reroll recovery are now named, and the read-only fixture
proves 4-of-4 agreement for one historical block. The ACI Arena workload
deployment, GenVM source probe, provider-independence claim, and real GenLayer
finality committee are still open evidence items. Solidity parity is a Phase 6
implementation acceptance check, not evidence authority by itself.
Until those rows have live authoritative evidence, `Evidence authenticity`,
`Evidence availability`, and `Full lifecycle` remain open and no production
contract or frontend may be created.
